Each frame keeps its own tracing picture, so you can line up frames from another animation and sketch against them. 100% fits that picture inside the sprite. Hold Space and drag to move the canvas. The picture is saved with the project and left out of exports.

Shortcuts

P pen · V mirror · B bucket · A replace color · E eraser

L line · R rectangle · C ellipse · M move · U lighten · T dither · O pick · S select

Left click draws the primary color. Right click draws the secondary. A transparent color erases.

[ ] pen size · X swap colors · Alt+click picks a color · Shift with the picker samples the sprite instead of the trace · Alt+G grid · Alt+L layer preview

Hold Space and drag to move the canvas anywhere · middle-drag does the same · + − zoom · 0 fit · wheel zooms at the cursor · Shift+arrows nudges the view

Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+Y redo · Ctrl+S save · Ctrl+O open · Ctrl+C/X/V copy, cut, paste a selection

Arrows nudge a selection. Delete clears it. Escape stamps a floating selection back down.

Shift on the line tool snaps to a clean slope. Shift on a rectangle or ellipse makes a square or circle.
